Competitive Shooting

Hit the Stage

Get Started in USPSA

Seven steps from finding a match to your first scorecard. Read the full safety guide before you go — it's mandatory.

1 · Be Safe2 · Avoid DQ3 · Have Fun
Step 01 · Find a Match

Sign Up on PractiScore

PractiScore is where matches are listed, registered for and scored. Your results land here after every match.

  • Create a free account at practiscore.com or in the app
  • Search for matches near Buffalo / WNY and open the club's match page
  • Read the details — date, start time, round count, fees and club rules
  • Register and choose your division (Carry Optics, Limited Optics, Production…)
  • Squad with someone you know if you can — first matches are easier with a friend
Open PractiScore →
Step 02 · Know the Sport

Know What You're Getting Into

USPSA is hit factor competition: move through a course of fire, engage multiple targets, shoot from unconventional positions and reload on the clock — all with your finger on the frame between shots.

  • Built for seasoned handgun shooters attending their first action pistol match
  • Brand new to handguns? Keep renting range time and taking classes first
  • Reading the safety guide in full is mandatory before your first match
Read the Safety Guide →
Step 03 · Gear Up

Pack the Range Bag

  • Eyes + ears — plugs, muffs or electronic
  • Safe, reliable semi-auto — 9mm, .380, .40, .45, 10mm. No rimfire. NYS compliant
  • 3–5 magazines (10-round max in NYS) + 2–4 mag pouches
  • Rigid Kydex holster, OWB or IWB
  • 75–100 rounds, a dedicated gun bag, a brass bag and clean sneakers

🚫 No pocket, sticky, soft or leather holsters. No exceptions.

Step 04 · Arrive

Check In & Find the Safe Table

  • Check in at the counter, pay $10 cash and complete the match waiver
  • Unload your carry gun at the "Unload Here" sign (or in your car)
  • The Safe Table is the only place to handle your gun — no live ammo there
  • Holster an unloaded pistol and leave it there until the RO says "Make Ready"

Introduce yourself to the RO, Match Director and your squad.

Step 05 · Plan

Walk the Stage

After the safety meeting, the RO reads the Written Stage Brief. You get 5 minutes to walk it — and you shoot each stage once.

  • Start position and hand / gun start
  • Round count and target count
  • Target order, movement path and reloads
  • Special rules — mandatory reload, strong / weak hand
  • Mentally rehearse the run
Step 06 · Shoot Safe

Know the Range Commands

  • Make Ready → load, hand on grip while you rehearse, hand off when ready
  • Are You Ready? → Standby → Beep → run the stage
  • Unload & Show Clear → drop mag, lock slide open, show the chamber
  • STOP → finger off the trigger, freeze, wait for the RO
  • Finger outside the guard on every move and reload

⚠️ Break the 180° — or point the muzzle at yourself or the RO — and it's an immediate DQ.

Step 07 · Score & Improve

Hit Factor Is King

Two rounds on every cardboard target unless the brief says otherwise. Points divided by time — speed and accuracy.

  • Wait for the RO to call the score before you paste
  • Verify your hits and approve your score on the tablet
  • Results hit Practiscore by dinner time
  • First match goal: be safe and shoot Alphas efficiently
